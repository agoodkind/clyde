# Superseded generic LMS ingestion cutover plan

The [shared search coordination plan](2026-09-27-shared-search-coordination.md) controls execution. The [Clyde design](../specs/2026-09-27-embedded-search-design.md) defines current ingestion behavior. The [projection](2026-09-27-embedded-conversation-projection.md), [ingestion](2026-09-27-embedded-conversation-ingestion.md), and [runtime](2026-09-27-embedded-conversation-runtime.md) plans define Clyde work. The historical LMS RPC, manifest replacement, and production cutover steps do not apply. Selected-content counts, unchanged-pass work, clean-install stages, and full-corpus estimates remain acceptance requirements in the current plans.
